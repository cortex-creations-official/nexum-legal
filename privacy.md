# Privacy Policy for Nexum

**Effective Date:** August 16, 2026

Nexum ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we handle user data.

### 1. Information We Collect
* **Account Information:** Mobile phone number provided during account creation via Truecaller SDK verification.
* **Profile Data:** User-provided display name.
* **Messages:** Sent and received messages routed through Firebase Firestore services to enable real-time messaging functionality.

### 2. How We Use Data
* To verify your unique account and identity.
* To facilitate real-time chat and user communication.

### 3. Data Storage & Security
Your data is securely stored using Firebase Cloud Services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.
